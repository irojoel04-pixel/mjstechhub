MY STORY

Engineering, education and practical problem-solving.

My name is Irogbanyo-John Ayebatonbara Joel, professionally known as MJ. I am a detail-oriented Mechanical Engineer with interests and practical experience spanning mechanical CAD design, technical writing, Python programming and education. I studied Mechanical Engineering at the Federal University Otuoke as a beneficiary of the Otonye Efemo Foundation university scholarship.

I was born in Egweama Kingdom, Brass Local Government Area, Bayelsa State, my mother’s community, and raised in Twon Brass after my dad Mr. John Irogbanyo and mom Mrs. Irogbanyo Tonprenyo moved there. My education began at Sunny Goli Memorial School in the early 2000s, continued at Community Primary School Two, Twon Brass, in 2008, and then at Community Secondary School Beletiema from 2010 to 2016. I gained admission to the Federal University Otuoke in 2019 and graduated in 2025 after academic delays that included national ASUU strikes.

PROFESSIONAL CAPABILITIES

Skills built through practice, projects and communication.

Mechanical CAD Design

I use Autodesk Fusion 360 to model mechanical components, assemblies and functional engineering concepts. My project work includes a Solar Hybrid Fish Dryer and a Motorized Screw Jack. I developed my CAD skills through practical training at Clintonel Innovations Centre in Aba.

02

I am developing Python solutions through practical exercises and small projects, building on my engineering mindset and interest in using software to solve problems. I present this as an actively developing capability alongside my CAD and writing work.

WORK & TEACHING EXPERIENCE

Teaching has been a consistent part of my journey.

I worked at God’s Light International School in Twon Brass from 2015 to 2016 and later worked at Holy Trinity Academy in 2017. During university breaks, I also took up work with different organisations. These experiences strengthened my communication, organisation and ability to explain ideas to learners.

During my time at the Federal University Otuoke, I organised free tutorials for newly admitted 100-level students. Through these sessions, I helped students prepare for foundational science, mathematics and calculation-based courses, including MTH 101, MTH 102, PHY 101, PHY 102, CHM 101, CHM 102 and Business Mathematics for Management Sciences students.

I founded MJ’s Tutorial, a campus tutorial initiative created to help first-year students prepare for examinations and build a stronger foundation for their transition into 200 level. The initiative reflected my belief that students benefit when difficult concepts are explained patiently and practically.

INDUSTRIAL TRAINING

From engineering theory to practical design.

The experience helped me connect engineering principles with practical design and fabrication challenges and contributed to my proficiency in mechanical modelling with Autodesk Fusion 360.
