02 / MY APPROACH

Good technical writing should make complex work easier to understand.

01

Understand the subject

Before writing, I focus on understanding the technical subject, requirements and intended purpose of the document.

02

Organise the information

Technical information is arranged in a logical structure so that each section supports the overall document.

03

Communicate clearly

The goal is to communicate technical ideas accurately without making the document unnecessarily difficult to understand.
